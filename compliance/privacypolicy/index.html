Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 1, 2025

Welcome to ChatGenius! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect any information you may provide while using our website: https://msblight1.github.io/chatgenius/ ("Site").

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) from users who visit our Site. The Site is a static web application and does not require account registration, cookies, or third-party tracking scripts.

2. Non-Personal Information

We may automatically collect limited non-personal information such as:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Date and time of visit
  • Referring website

This data may be collected via GitHub Pages' hosting analytics or the browser itself, but we do not use or store this data ourselves.

3. Use of Information

Since we do not collect or store any personal data, we do not use your data for marketing, profiling, or analytics.

4. Third-Party Services

This Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may collect limited technical data for operational purposes. Please refer to GitHub's Privacy Statement for more details.

The chatbot functionality uses the OpenAI API, please refer to the API provider's privacy policy. We do not store or share your inputs or outputs with any external service beyond what is required for basic chatbot function.

5. Cookies

We do not use cookies or local storage to track users.

6. Children's Privacy

This Site is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

Even though we do not collect personal information, we use GitHub Pages' secure HTTPS protocol to encrypt data in transit.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the site maintainer via the GitHub repository:

https://github.com/msblight1/chatgenius
